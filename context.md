The project is aimed at:
1. implementing the stress-fluctuation formula for the time- (or frequency-) dependent nonlinear elastic moduli (third-order elastic constants) using the data generated by the equilibrium molecular dynamics simulations;
2. checking this formula against nonequilibrium MD simulations where the body is subject to a sudden strain and the stress responce is monitored in time. 

The known formula for the linear elastic moduli (second order elastic constants), $C$, and the nonlinear elastic constants, $N$:
$$\begin{align*}
C_{ijkl}(t-t_1) =&\, \langle C^{BK}_{ijkl}(t)\rangle_0 - \beta V_0 \langle\sigma_{ij}(t) \sigma_{kl}(t)\rangle_0 + \beta V_0 \langle\sigma_{ij}(t) \sigma_{kl}(t_1)\rangle_0,\\
N_{ijklmn}(t-t_1, t-t_2) =&\, \langle N^{BK}_{ijklmn}(t)\rangle_0 + \frac{\beta V_0}2 \langle C^{BK}_{ijkl}(t) (\sigma_{mn}(t_2) - \sigma_{mn}(t)\rangle_0 \nonumber\\
&+ \frac{\beta V_0}2 \langle C^{BK}_{ijmn}(t) (\sigma_{kl}(t_1) - \sigma_{kl}(t)\rangle_0\nonumber\\
&+ \frac{\beta V_0}{2} \langle\sigma_{ij}(t) \bigl( C^{BK}_{klmn}(\max\{t_1,t_2\}) -  C^{BK}_{klmn}(t)\bigr)\rangle_0 \nonumber\\
&+ \frac{\beta^2 V_0^2}{2} \langle\sigma_{ij}(t) (\sigma_{kl}(t_1) - \sigma_{kl}(t)) (\sigma_{mn}(t_2) - \sigma_{mn}(t))\rangle_0,
\end{align*}$$
where $C^{BK}$ is the Born-kinetic term and $N^{BK}$ is the analogous term for the nonlinear moduli.
